Drawings

The mind is the blank canvas on which every amazing and timeless design begins.

Drawings are the perfect tool in effective communication. As the old adage states, “a picture is worth a 1,000 words”. This is very true, but when something only exists as an idea, it is a sketch, a rendering or a detailed construction drawing that becomes the perfect form of communication. Just as the land is a blank canvas on which to create beautiful and emotional compositions and spaces, the mind is the blank canvas on which every amazing and timeless design begins.

Master plan

“Being able to use a drawing as a conduit to relay an idea is just as important as the idea itself.”

Site plan

“…not only what the idea will look like, but in perfect scale and relation.”

Construction drawing

“Reality starts to unfold and the design ideas start to take shape.”

A drawing is your bridge to the wonderful world of design.

When an idea is sketched out, refined into a rendered drawing and then developed into an accurate detailed construction drawing, the proper steps are taken to ensure that not only the design intent is met, but construction mistakes are essentially eliminated.

The Practice

Designing Michigan landscapes since 1987.

Michigan native Charles Loughrey decided at the young age of 15 that he wanted to become a Landscape Architect.

A Registered Landscape Architect since 1992, Charles has earned numerous awards for the design of over 100 landscape projects. His firm specializes in landscape architecture and interior design, as well as designing specialty components and site amenities. In 2009 he was in the first Green Roofs for Healthy Cities class to become Green Roof Professionals.
